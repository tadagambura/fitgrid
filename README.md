# FitGrid

**Your wardrobe in your pocket**

> #24 of 32 · Wave 2: Recurring keepers · Used: Daily · Harder · Revenue potential: Medium · Social, Play & Style

## What it is

Photograph your clothes to build a digital wardrobe, put together outfits and plan what to wear by day, weather or occasion. See what you actually wear and what's just taking up space.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Style content is huge on social media and users pay for convenience. It has the most moving parts, so it comes last, once the shared foundation is mature.

## Who it's for

Fashion-conscious users who want to wear more of what they already own.

**The problem:** People forget what they own, rebuy similar items and struggle to plan outfits.

## Version one

- Photograph items into a digital wardrobe
- Background removal for clean item images
- Build outfits
- Outfit calendar
- Most and least worn

**Example:** On Sunday a user plans a week of work outfits from clothes they already own.

## Later

- Weather-based suggestions
- Cost per wear (links to WorthIt)
- Pack from your wardrobe (links to PackRight)

## Animation and tone: five signature animations

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- Outfit built: the clothes snap together and strut down a runway
- Outfit of the day: the mirror winks
- Unworn for 90 days: a forgotten jumper waves from the back of the wardrobe
- Cost-per-wear drops: the price tag shrinks
- Week planned: the hangers do the can-can

**Line we don't cross:** Never comments on bodies or looks.
